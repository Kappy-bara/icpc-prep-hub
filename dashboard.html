Profile

Log in with your Codeforces handle to track roadmap progress, sync solves, and earn points. Logging in proves you own the handle (below) — there's no separate "save, then verify" step, and no way to preview another handle's stats as your own. First login creates your local profile; logging back in later with the same handle restores it exactly as you left it, even if you'd switched to a different handle in between.
