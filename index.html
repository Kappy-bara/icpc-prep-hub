ICPC Prep Hub

An open-source companion for ICPC prep — regionals through World Finals. Works entirely offline, local-only, by default; sign in on the Dashboard only if you want your data synced across devices.

Your progress

Dashboard
Account & cloud sync, profile, timeline pacing, your streak, and a roadmap progress summary
Codeforces
Sync solves and compare your tag mix against real sampled Codeforces players, plus accuracy, rating trajectory, and recommendations
Roadmap
99 topics across 8 subjects, from foundations to World-Finals-level techniques
Self-rule
Points, streaks, rewards, and a breakdown of what you've earned and spent
