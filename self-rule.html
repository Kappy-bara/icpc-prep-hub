How points work

Base points are round(rating / 100) for rated problems, or a flat 5 for unrated ones. Solves tagged with one of your focus tags (—) earn ×1.5 points. Every solve is worth at least 1 point.

Your gamification start date is not set. Solves from that date onward earn points; anything solved before it is logged for stats on the Codeforces page but scores 0 — so importing your whole history doesn't hand you a windfall. Change either of these on the Dashboard.
